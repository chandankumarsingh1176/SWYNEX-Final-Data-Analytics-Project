SWYNEX FINAL DATA ANALYTICS PROJECT
Titanic Survival Analysis - Key Findings Report
============================================================

OVERALL STATISTICS
- Total Passengers : 891
- Total Survivors  : 342
- Survival Rate    : 38.4%

KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS
----------------------------------------
1. Gender was the strongest predictor of survival.
   Women survived at ~74% vs Men at ~19%.

2. Socio-economic status (Passenger Class) significantly
   affected survival chances (1st: ~63%, 3rd: ~24%).

3. Children and women had priority access to lifeboats
   ('Women and children first' policy).

4. Higher fare passengers had better survival odds due to
   better cabin locations and access to lifeboats.

5. Most passengers were young adults (19-35 years old).

6. Passengers traveling with family had slightly better
   survival rates than those traveling alone.
